scan a whole shelf

Barcodes are one at a time. This reads the whole shelf at once. Take a photo down the aisle and it will tell you which brands in shot are worth picking up, before you have touched anything.

take a photo of the shelf, or pick one from your camera roll

This reads text off the packaging in your photo, so it works best on a straight-on, well-lit shot where the brand names are legible. Fancy fonts, glare and awkward angles will trip it up. It only finds brands already on my brand check list, so a blank result usually means I have not covered those brands yet rather than that they are bad. Nothing leaves your phone, the reading happens in your browser.
