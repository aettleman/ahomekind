who actually gets your money

Nobody can tell you what percentage of a company's profit funds animal testing. They do not publish it, and anyone who gives you that number made it up.

What is knowable is where the money lands. A lot of cruelty-free brands are owned by companies that are not. Whether that is a dealbreaker is your call, but you should get to make it knowingly.
