popular products, kinder swaps

Some of the biggest names on the shelf are still not cruelty-free - not because of a rumour or an old boycott, but because their parent company sells into markets like mainland China, where animal testing on cosmetics can still be legally required, and hasn't adopted a policy that rules it out everywhere. Here are the ones you've probably got in your bathroom or under your sink right now, each paired with a genuinely certified alternative that does the same job.

Every brand below, on both sides, is pulled straight from my brand check database - nothing here is a one-off claim, you can look each one up yourself.
