This listing doesn't cover Avon in the US or Canada. Avon there is a different company and isn't certified cruelty-free. Avon International is certified cruelty-free by Leaping Bunny / Cruelty Free International. Its listing names Africa & The Middle East, Asia, Europe and South & Central America. Avon confirmed to me by email that all of its products are vegan, but it has no vegan certification. Regent LP, an investment company rather than a manufacturer, owns Avon in Europe, Asia and Africa (it bought Avon International from Natura, reported as closed in January 2026), and Natura keeps Latin America. Regent has no animal testing policy of its own that applies to Avon. What counts is each brand's own policy. Not sure which Avon you're buying? Check that it comes from one of Avon's sites outside North America, such as avon.uk.com in the UK or avon.co.in in India, and look for the Leaping Bunny logo. Avon's site for the US and Canada, avon.com, isn't covered. Avon's India site says all of its beauty products are approved under the Leaping Bunny programme.
