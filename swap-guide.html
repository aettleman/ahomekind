why these ones aren't cruelty-free

Not because of a rumour or an old boycott. Their parent companies sell into markets like mainland China, where animal testing on cosmetics can still be legally required, and haven't adopted a policy ruling it out everywhere. Every brand below, on both sides, comes straight from the brand check, so you can look each one up yourself.
