Games where kindness is the whole point

Both games star the same animals: Peggy the pig, Rubie the rabbit, Rosie the rat, Callie the cow, Shellie the sheep and friends. There's no farming in either. Nobody makes wool, milk or eggs. Animals are there because they're safe, and the game is about keeping them happy.

They work on phones, tablets and laptops, there's nothing to download, and nothing to sign up for. Every animal fact links to where it came from, so you can read the research yourself.
