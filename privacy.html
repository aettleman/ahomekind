privacy policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026.

This page explains what happens to your information when you use this website, and in particular when you sign up to the mailing list. It's written in plain language rather than dense legal text, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

who runs this site

a home kind is run by an individual, not a registered company — there's no company name, registered address, or company number to list here because none exists. This site doesn't currently generate any income.

mailing list sign-up

If you enter your email address in the "stay in the loop" box (at the bottom of most pages) and tick the consent box, that email address is sent to this site's email newsletter provider so it can send you the updates you asked for.

What's collected: your email address, and the fact that you opted in (including the date/time), which is kept as a consent record.

Why: so you can receive the newsletter, and so there's a record showing you actually asked for it (a legal requirement for email marketing).

What it's used for: sending the occasional newsletter described next to the sign-up box — new journal posts, brand-check updates, and cruelty-free finds. It is not used for anything else, and it is not sold or given to advertisers.

Who processes it: the email is stored and sent by Buttondown, a third-party email newsletter platform, acting as data processor for this list. You can read Buttondown's own privacy policy at buttondown.com/privacy.

Unsubscribing: every email includes an unsubscribe link, provided automatically by Buttondown. Clicking it removes you from the list immediately. You can also unsubscribe by emailing hello@ahomekind.com.

How long it's kept: for as long as you remain subscribed. Buttondown's own privacy policy states that personal data is kept only for as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected for, and is deleted or anonymised once there's no ongoing legitimate reason to keep it — they don't publish an exact number of days or months this takes. If you unsubscribe, a minimal record of that is likely kept for a short time (so you aren't accidentally re-added), consistent with standard email marketing practice.

other data collected by the site

This site does not use tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising scripts at the time of writing. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log a basic request (such as your IP address) as part of serving the font files — this is standard for any site using web fonts and is not used by this site to identify you.

If you email hello@ahomekind.com directly, that email and your address are kept as part of normal correspondence, for as long as is reasonably needed to deal with your query.

your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to: know what data is held about you; ask for a copy of it; ask for it to be corrected or deleted; withdraw consent at any time (e.g. by unsubscribing); and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you think your data hasn't been handled properly.

To exercise any of these rights, contact hello@ahomekind.com.

As this site isn't currently trading or earning income, it isn't required to register or pay a fee with the ICO at this time (per the ICO's own self-assessment). This will be reviewed if that changes.

contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@ahomekind.com.

keeping this up to date

If this site starts earning income, or adds analytics, cookies, or advertising tools in future, this policy will be updated to reflect that — including re-checking whether ICO registration becomes required.

stay in the loop

occasional updates, new journal posts, brand-check updates and cruelty-free finds — no inbox spam, unsubscribe whenever you like.
