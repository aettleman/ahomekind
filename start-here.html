start here

New to this? Start wherever feels least overwhelming. You don't need to overhaul everything at once, that's not how any of this sticks.

beauty & skincare

Usually the easiest place to begin, since nobody needs animal-tested shampoo. Check the ratings page for brands you can trust, or look for the Leaping Bunny or PETA bunny logo (verified via the certifier's own site, not just the bottle).

food & kitchen

Going fully vegan is a genuinely thriving way to eat, not a compromise. Start with the meals you already make often and find the swap, rather than overhauling your whole kitchen overnight.

fashion & accessories

Leather, wool, silk, and down are the main ones to look out for. Look for certified vegan materials, and check the difference between "vegan leather" (can still be plastic-heavy) and genuinely sustainable alternatives.

household & cleaning

An easy swap most people overlook entirely. See the ratings page for cruelty-free options across the major supermarkets and dedicated brands.

what to actually look for

A bunny on a bottle means nothing unless someone's checked it. These are the three worth trusting, click through, search the brand, see for yourself:

· Leaping Bunny
· Cruelty Free International
· PETA's Beauty Without Bunnies
