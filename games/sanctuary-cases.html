      About Sanctuary Cases

      Sanctuary Cases is a free logic puzzle you can play in your browser in a few minutes. Overnight, everyone at the sanctuary has moved. Read the clues, place each animal in the right spot, and check your answer.

      Clues that are true stories

      The clues are built on real animal behaviour: sheep that remember faces, rats that play hide and seek, cows with best friends. When you solve the case, you find out which clue was a true story, with a link to the research behind it.
