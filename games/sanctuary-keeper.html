    About Sanctuary Keeper

    Sanctuary Keeper is a free, cosy animal game you play right here in your browser, on your phone or laptop. You start with Peggy the pig and an empty field. Give her a straw bed and something to play with, and she's happy. Happy animals bring visitors, visitors leave hearts, and hearts build more of the sanctuary.

    How to play

    Seventeen animals arrive over time, from Rubie the rabbit and Rosie the rat to Hector the horse and Olive the barn owl. Each one needs a couple of things to feel at home. Tap the little jobs that pop up around the field, answer animal questions for bonus hearts, cuddle everyone, and when a camera appears, snap the moment for your album. Any snap can become a postcard to save and share.

    Real facts, no sign-up

    Every animal fact in the game is true and links to its source. Your sanctuary saves on your device, so there's no account to make. Fancy something quicker? Try Sanctuary Cases, a short logic puzzle with the same animals, or check whether a brand tests on animals.
