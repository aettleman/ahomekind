check the ingredients

A cruelty-free certified brand can still sell a product that isn't vegan. The barcode scanner already checks this automatically when the product database has an ingredients list on file - this page is for everything else: use it when a barcode comes back with no ingredients data, or when you'd rather just photograph what's in your hand. Either way, whatever turns up is named plainly - not left as an INCI name you'd have to look up yourself.

take a photo of the ingredients list, or pick one from your camera roll

For the clearest read: fill the frame with just the ingredients text, hold the phone straight-on rather than at an angle, and make sure there's no glare across the panel. This only recognises ingredient names already in my dictionary and can miss things a photo reads badly - it's a helpful first pass, not a guarantee, and it's not a substitute for a brand's own vegan claim. "Fragrance"/"parfum" can legally hide undisclosed ingredients that no OCR tool can see. Nothing leaves your phone - the reading happens in your browser.
