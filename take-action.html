Each one is a ready-made letter to your MP on a specific, current gap in UK animal welfare law. MPs are obliged to read letters from their own constituents, and volume genuinely registers. Read a letter, change anything you like, then pop your postcode in underneath: it copies the letter for you and opens WriteToThem, a free, independent service that finds your MP and sends it to them. Just paste it into the message box. Two minutes, no expertise needed.

These letters go to your MP, not your local councillor. Councils have no say over animal testing or farming law, so a councillor can only pass it on.

cosmetics testing

Cosmetics testing is banned in the UK. Testing the ingredients inside them isn’t.

The UK banned animal testing for cosmetics in 1998, and for cosmetic ingredients in 1998 as well. But ingredients used in cosmetics can still be tested on animals when they're assessed under chemicals regulation (REACH) rather than as cosmetics, which is the gap this asks about. Licences of that kind have been granted in recent years.

Dear [MP's name],

I am writing as one of your constituents in [your constituency].

The UK is rightly proud of having banned animal testing for cosmetics and cosmetic ingredients. However, I understand that ingredients destined for cosmetics can still be tested on animals when they are assessed for chemical safety under REACH rather than under cosmetics rules, and that licences permitting this have been granted in recent years.

I would like to know what the Government's position is on closing that gap, so that the ban means in practice what the public understands it to mean. I would also welcome your view on the funding and regulatory acceptance of non-animal testing methods, which remain the route to ending these procedures altogether.

The Government's own roadmap for reducing animal testing, backed by £60 million towards non-animal alternatives, already aims to phase out several other forms of animal testing by 2026 and 2027. I'd like to know why cosmetic ingredient testing under REACH isn't included in that same commitment.

I would be grateful if you could let me know your own view, and whether you intend to raise this with the relevant minister.

Thank you for your time.

Yours sincerely,
[your name]
[your address, including postcode]

a note on accuracy

Anything on this page that describes the law is written from the actual rules as far as I can establish them, and I have tried to keep what is fact separate from what is my own opinion. If you spot something out of date or wrong, please tell me, and I will correct it.
